# TFM ATD: the moments between the drafts

Working website copy based on the historic-moments channel. This is a community
story to sit alongside the verified ATD 1–112 draft archive, not a complete
chronology of those drafts.

## Suggested introduction

Every ATD ends with a result. The community remembers what happened on the way
there: the matchup everyone argued about, the trade someone refused to let go,
the comeback that seemed impossible, and the draft pick that became a running
joke.

The historic-moments archive captures that side of TFM. Its posts connect the
competition to the people playing it. They show why the story needs more than a
list of champions: each draft adds another moment that members recognise, revisit,
and carry into the next one.

## The debates that became part of the language

A screenshot dated 14 April 2024 preserves the community rallying around Danny
Green defending Michael Jordan. Multiple members repeat the same matchup claim,
turning one basketball argument into a shared moment.

Use this as an early example of ATD's personality: the conviction behind a lineup
choice can become as memorable as the result itself. It is evidence of the
community's discussion, not proof of the basketball claim.

## Suggested connection to the website

Pair each draft's factual record with a small collection of sourced community
moments. A reader should be able to move from the draft's rules and results to
the discussion, comeback, or joke associated with it. The bot directory can explain
the tools members use today; these posts explain the community those tools serve.

The historic channel alone cannot date the beginning of ATD or populate every
draft from 1 to 112. Draft numbers and event dates must come from explicit source
evidence or the draft archive. Many posts are retrospective, especially the
initial September 2024 batch.
